Browser History

The top banner on Bandcamp, along with all the links on the page, will turn green or red based on your Chrome browsing history. This helps you identify if you've visited these pages before.


  Play History

Tracks will display in one of ten shades of red, based on your play history.

Use the following buttons to manage your data. All data is lost when the extension is uninstalled.




  Tracks In Common

See how many tracks you have in common with other users by hovering over their thumbnail in the track or album view.

Bandcamp will rate-limit your traffic if too many fan thumbnails are loaded at once. POWERDIGGER now throttles the requests and retries on rate-limit, but very large fan lists may still take a while.

  External Buttons

Buttons appear for SoundCloud and Buy Music Club on Bandcamp track / album pages.


  BMC Keyboard Navigation

On Buy Music Club, arrow keys navigate playback: ↑ restart, ↓ next, ← / → seek 10 s.
